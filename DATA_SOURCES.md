Access, use and redistribution are governed by the original repositories. Obtain raw data there; this release contains derived scores and analysis records only. Model weights are also obtained separately. Original dataset and model publications are listed in paper/nature_portfolio/references.bib.

The exact saved files consumed by the reporting scripts are enumerated in paper/nature_portfolio/build_manifest.json. Supplementary Data 1 preserves every original comparison row (T0001–T0904), including missing values, negative results and the original multiple-comparison families. No new correction across the combined inventory is implied. Numerical source paths resolve relative to the repository root.
