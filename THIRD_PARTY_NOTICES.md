# Third-party materials

No raw EEG, pretrained model weights or third-party model implementation is included in this release. The pinned upstream-source download helper preserves the original downloaded bytes, source URLs and hashes; consult each upstream license before use.

- CBraMod: https://github.com/wjq-learning/CBraMod — source MIT license (Jiquan Wang, 2025); pinned asset manifest in configs/assets.json.
- LaBraM: https://github.com/935963004/LaBraM — source MIT license (Weibang Jiang, 2024); pinned commit in configs/cross_model_x.yaml.
- REVE: https://github.com/elouayas/reve_eeg and https://huggingface.co/brain-bzh/reve-base — model-specific REVE Responsible Use License and upstream source terms; inspect the source manifest and the upstream license. The original analysis-code MIT license does not replace these terms.
- Dataset locations and access terms are listed in DATA_SOURCES.md. Public subject identifiers are retained solely to join saved scores and split definitions; no participant names or contact details are included.

The source protocols may refer to local execution or authorization stages. They are research provenance, not credentials or permission to run a job on the authors' infrastructure.
